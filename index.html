About Me

I'm Kavish Soningra, a Computer Science student at Texas Christian University, originally from Mumbai, India. Traveling to more than twenty countries has shaped how I see the world and taught me the value of communication across cultures.

At TCU, I bring together my interests in technology and writing. Coding pushes me to solve complex problems, while writing helps me reflect and communicate ideas clearly. Recently, I co-founded a health tech startup and now serve as its CTO, working on solutions to centralize health records.

Beyond academics, I enjoy music, reading, traveling, playing pickleball, and DJing. I also speak four languages, which helps me connect with people from diverse backgrounds. Stories of resilience and creativity, whether found in books, films, or real experiences, continue to inspire me. Looking ahead, I hope to grow both as a technologist and a writer, using my skills to create impact and contribute meaningfully to the conversations that shape our world.

My journey in writing has taught me that effective communication requires understanding your audience and adapting your approach to different rhetorical situations. Through this e-portfolio, I aim to showcase my growth as both a writer and critical thinker, demonstrating how diverse experiences have shaped my perspective on storytelling and discourse.
